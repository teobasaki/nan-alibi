제약이유
얼굴이 한 프레임도 나오면 안 된다시드마다 범인이 다르다(용의자 5명, 성별·나이·직업 전부 달라짐). 얼굴이 보이면 그 판과 안 맞는다.
성별이 읽히는 실루엣도 금지머리 길이·체형으로 읽힌다. 중절모 + 롱코트 + 역광으로 지운다.
폭력 묘사 금지문이 닫힌 뒤는 비운다. 게임이 이미 "오답이면 진범을 밝히지 않는다"는 규율을 갖고 있고 같은 결이다.
화면 안에 글자 금지게임이 한국어 자막을 얹는다. 호수(1204)조차 새기지 않는다 — 시드마다 바뀔 수 있다.

1A
SCENE CONTEXT
Late night on an upper floor of a 1960s hotel. An empty corridor. A gloved hand brings a plain hotel keycard to a door reader, and the reader's lamp turns from red to green. Nobody's face is ever in frame.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@corridor — Controls environment, materials and mood only; reference framing not inherited.
@door — Controls the door and card reader design only; studio sheet background and layout NOT inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: deep burgundy patterned carpet, its weave catching the low light. Midground: the right-hand wall with heavy dark wood doors and brass handles, the nearest door 3 m from camera. Background: the corridor recedes 18 m into near darkness, one fluorescent fixture at 12 m flickering unstably. Light comes only from the ceiling fixtures overhead and slightly behind camera. The gloved hand enters frame from the right edge in the later cuts and stays within 40 cm of the door.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
The first frame is an empty corridor: camera at 1.6 m height on the corridor centreline, doors receding along the right side, carpet filling the lower third. No people, no hands. Composition is a one-point perspective with the vanishing point slightly right of centre. The nearest door's brass handle sits at the right third line and is the landmark everything later is pinned to.

FORMAT MODE
Sequence of cuts, no timecodes — cuts only at the specified points, the camera does not cut on its own. CUT 1 holds longest and is completely still; CUT 2 is closer and calmer; CUT 3 is the shortest and snaps.

CUT 1 — wide, 63° FOV, camera 1.6 m high on the corridor centreline, static
The empty corridor holds. The distant fluorescent fixture stutters twice, dropping the far end of the corridor into darkness and bringing it back. Dust drifts slowly through the nearer light. Nothing else moves.

CUT 2 — HARD CUT to extreme close-up, 18° FOV, camera 1.5 m high, static, focused 30 cm from the subject
A hand in a thin dark leather glove holds a plain pale keycard between thumb and forefinger, lifted into the light. The glove leather creases across the knuckles as the grip settles. The card face is blank, catching a single soft highlight along its edge. Behind the hand the corridor falls away into unfocused darkness.

CUT 3 — HARD CUT to close-up, 29° FOV, camera 1.4 m high, static
The gloved hand brings the card to the brass-framed reader beside the door handle. The card touches the plate. The reader's small lamp shifts from deep red to green, and the green throws a faint cast onto the brass frame and the glove's fingertips. The hand holds still one moment longer, then begins to lower.

OPTICS
CUT 1 at 63° rectilinear, deep focus so the far corridor stays legible. CUT 2 at 18°, shallow focus with the corridor collapsed to soft darkness behind. CUT 3 at 29°, focus on the reader plate. Prime-lens character, gentle falloff at the edges, natural motion blur on the flicker. No drift mid-segment.

CAMERA
Locked on a tripod for all three cuts, no handheld movement. Camera height drops from 1.6 m to 1.4 m across the cuts. Distance: 6 m, then 0.3 m, then 0.8 m. Focus is set before each cut and does not hunt.

ACTION
The flicker in CUT 1 runs on its own rhythm and is not synchronised to anything. In CUT 3 the card contact and the lamp change happen as one continuous motion — the hand does not pause and then wait for the light; the light changes while the hand is still settling. No frozen poses; the glove keeps micro-adjusting through the whole cut.

PHYSICS
The card has the stiffness and slight flex of thin plastic. The glove compresses where fingers press. Contact shadow appears under the card where it meets the reader plate. Dust motes fall at real gravity, slowly, and drift sideways where air moves.

LIGHTING
Overhead fluorescent, 4000K, cool and slightly green in the fixtures, falling off fast to the walls. The corridor sits about two stops under a normal exposure. The failing fixture at 12 m drops and returns unevenly. In CUT 3 the reader's red lamp reads as a small deep crimson point, then becomes green at roughly the same brightness. Haze visible at 12 m depth, about 15%.

AUDIO
The low hum of a ventilation system throughout. The failing fluorescent adds a faint electrical buzz that cuts out and returns with the flicker. In CUT 2 the small dry sound of a card sliding free of a pocket. In CUT 3 a single soft electronic contact click and the mechanical thunk of a bolt withdrawing inside the door. No music. No voices.

STYLE
Photoreal, 4K, anamorphic, fine grain, real-time throughout. Warm amber and deep burgundy grade against near-black shadows, low saturation everywhere except the carpet and the brass. Blacks stay dense and never lift.

POSITIVE LOCKS
No face, no head, no shoulders, no body appears in any frame — only a gloved hand from the wrist forward. Every surface stays free of writing, numbers and symbols; the brass plate beside the door stays blank. The keycard stays plain and unmarked. Exactly one hand in the entire generation. The door stays closed for the whole clip. Objects hold stable identity frame to frame, moving only when the hand moves them. The card reader stays the exact size and position established by @door. SCENE CONTEXT
Late night on an upper floor of a 1960s hotel. A door opens and a figure steps into the room; the door closes and the corridor holds on it; then the door opens again and the figure walks away down the corridor. The figure is never identifiable — always backlit, always from behind, face never in frame.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@corridor — Controls environment, materials and mood only; reference framing not inherited.
@door — Controls the door and card reader design only; studio sheet background and layout NOT inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: deep burgundy patterned carpet. Midground: the door on the right wall, 3 m from camera, with warm light behind it once it opens. Background: the corridor recedes 18 m into darkness, one fluorescent fixture at 12 m unstable. Two light sources oppose each other — cool ceiling fluorescent above and slightly behind camera, and warm room light spilling from the open doorway across the carpet. The figure's path runs from the doorway to the corridor's vanishing point.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
The first frame is a wide of the corridor with the door already swinging inward: camera 1.6 m high on the corridor centreline, the door at the right third line 3 m away, a widening wedge of warm light falling across the burgundy carpet toward camera. A figure in a long dark coat and a brimmed hat stands in the doorway, seen from behind, silhouetted against the warm interior, shoulders squared to the room. Distance from camera to figure 3.2 m. The figure's back fills roughly a fifth of frame height.

FORMAT MODE
Sequence of cuts, no timecodes — cuts only at the specified points, the camera does not cut on its own. CUT 2 holds longest and is the still centre of the clip; CUT 1 and CUT 3 carry the movement.

CUT 1 — wide, 63° FOV, camera 1.6 m high, slow dolly-in at 0.3 m/s
The door swings inward and warm light spreads across the carpet. The silhouetted figure steps through the doorway, one hand still on the frame, and moves out of the doorway into the room. The wedge of light narrows as the door begins to swing back.

CUT 2 — HARD CUT to medium, 47° FOV, camera 1.35 m high looking slightly up at the door, static
The door is closed. A thin line of warm light runs along the bottom edge of the door and does not change. The brass handle holds a single highlight. Nothing moves except dust drifting slowly through the cool overhead light and one stutter of the distant fluorescent. The corridor is completely still.

CUT 3 — HARD CUT to wide, 63° FOV, camera 1.6 m high, static
The door opens again and the same silhouetted figure steps out, pulls the door closed behind, and walks away from camera down the corridor. The wedge of warm light narrows to a line and disappears as the door seats. The figure recedes to below 8% of frame height and the corridor is left empty and still.

OPTICS
63° rectilinear on the wides, 47° on the door. Deep focus throughout so the corridor's far end stays legible. Prime-lens character, gentle edge falloff, natural motion blur on the door swing and the walk. No drift mid-segment.

CAMERA
Tripod for CUT 2 and CUT 3, entirely still. CUT 1 dollies forward at 0.3 m/s along the corridor centreline and stops before the cut. Camera height 1.6 m, 1.35 m, 1.6 m. Focus is set before each cut and does not hunt.

ACTION
In CUT 1 the door swing, the light spreading, and the figure's step all overlap — the figure is already moving while the door is still opening, and the door starts closing while the figure is still crossing the threshold. In CUT 3 the figure pulls the door shut and turns away in one continuous motion, then walks at an unhurried 1.1 m/s without looking back. No frozen poses; movement is continuous through every cut.

PERFORMANCE
The figure carries itself with unhurried control — shoulders level, no rush, no hesitation at the threshold. In CUT 3 the walk is slightly heavier than in CUT 1: the stride is a fraction shorter and the shoulders sit lower. One hand stays in a coat pocket. The head never turns toward camera.

PHYSICS
The door has real mass — it accelerates slowly and decelerates into the frame, and the latch seats with a small settle. The coat swings behind the figure's legs with a half-step delay. Contact shadow sits under both shoes at every footfall on the carpet. Dust falls at real gravity.

LIGHTING
Two opposed sources. Cool overhead fluorescent at 4000K from above and slightly behind camera, the corridor about two stops under normal exposure. Warm interior light at 2800K spilling from the open doorway, roughly one stop brighter than the corridor, throwing a hard-edged wedge across the burgundy carpet. In CUT 2 only a thin warm line under the door survives. The failing fixture at 12 m drops and returns unevenly. Haze visible at 12 m depth, about 15%.

AUDIO
Ventilation hum throughout and the faint electrical buzz of the failing fluorescent. In CUT 1 a heavy door swinging on its hinges and a single footfall on carpet. In CUT 2 the room tone alone, close and dry, with the hum sitting lower than before — the silence is the event. In CUT 3 the door pulling closed, the latch seating, and unhurried footsteps receding until they are gone. No music. No voices.

STYLE
Photoreal, 4K, anamorphic, fine grain, real-time throughout. Warm amber and deep burgundy grade against near-black shadows. The only saturated colours in frame are the carpet and the brass. Blacks stay dense and never lift.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The figure stays a silhouette at all times: seen from behind or in profile against light, brimmed hat on, face and neck fully in shadow, no facial feature ever visible. Build and gait stay neutral and unremarkable. The interior of the room stays dark and featureless beyond the doorway — nothing inside the room is legible at any point. Every surface stays free of writing, numbers and symbols; the brass plate beside the door stays blank. Exactly one person in the entire generation. Objects hold stable identity frame to frame. The door stays the exact size and position established by @door. The clip carries no injury, no struggle, and no aftermath — everything is conveyed by the closed door and the sound.
